## Linear Actuator Hardware Description
> Final addition to the project: a screw based linear actuator hardware

A linear actuator is designed using AutoDesk Fusion, and 3D-printed in ABS to bring life to the project. This is just for the puirpose of what hardware went into the physical actuator, since the design and render doesn't contain any other part except for what was 3D-printed.

### List of Parts:
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| actuator\_mech.f3d | In total 6 pieces, this contained the models of parts that are 3D printed and hold the actuator togther |
| Stepper Motor | NEMA17 200 steps/rev Stepper Motor |
| Snap Switch | Homing Switch, mounted near the beginning of movement region | 
| Lead Screw and Nut | Length 400 mm, Pitch 8 mm |
| Shaft Coupling | 5 x 8 mm, connects the motor shaft to lead screw |
| Cylindrical Rods | 2 parallel rods that hold the weight of moving platform |
| Linear Bearings | Travel on the rods and carry the platform, 1 for each rod |
| Axial Bearing | Holds the other end of the lead screw |
| Bolts, Nuts and Brass Inserts | They do what they usually do |
